# Legal research and publication blockers

**Draft research, not legal approval.** Initial distribution is broad international App Store availability, excluding the 27 EU member countries according to the owner. Japan and the United States are primary reference points. No country of operator establishment, governing law, legal identity, business address, personal phone, universal compliance or legal exemption is invented.

The site is a public legal-information site, not a checkout. That does not by itself settle how rules apply to the app's paid features, advertising, operator or actual App Store transaction structure. EU storefront exclusion does not determine all extraterritorial/privacy obligations, and the website remains publicly reachable.

## 1. App name versus legal operator

**MyPayce is a brand/app name used by an individual developer.** It must not be described as an incorporated company or automatically substituted for a legally required seller/controller name. The owner's legal identity and establishment are deliberately not collected or recorded in this public repository. An advisor may need them privately to assess obligations.

The draft contact `kaiomote10+MyPayce@gmail.com` is owner-approved and uses the same mailbox as feedback. Sharing the mailbox is not inherently a problem, but the owner must monitor it, recognize privacy/commercial-disclosure inquiries, protect correspondence and be able to fulfil valid requests. An email address alone is not a complete legal-identification arrangement.

## 2. Japan: Specified Commercial Transactions Act (特定商取引法)

Primary sources: CAA [mail-order guidance](https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/what/mailorder/) and [advertising Q&A, especially Q15–18](https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/qa/advertising.html), reviewed October 27, 2026.

CAA's guidance distinguishes an individual's legally recognized name/registered trade designation from an ordinary brand. It describes conditional omission of certain seller disclosures when the required notice is shown and the information can actually be provided promptly on request, in time for an informed application/purchase decision. This is not permission to withhold it forever. Other transaction disclosures have separate rules; a generic on-request sentence does not replace them all.

**Assessment, not a concluded exemption:** determine the seller/service-provider role under the actual Apple transaction arrangement and where advertising/purchase decisions occur. Do not assume Apple processes the payment, therefore the developer has no duties. A platform/virtual-office contact is not automatically usable: verify a qualifying arrangement rather than copying Apple's address or an unrelated address.

**Blockers:** approve an applicable operator-identification route; verify whether this app may use the conditional omission mechanism; establish a real pre-purchase request/response process; review price, payment, provision, renewal/cancellation, refund/return and supported-environment disclosures across the app, store and legal notice. Do not promise disclosure-on-request is operational now. No individual name, residential address or personal phone is to be entered in Git to solve these questions.

## 3. Japan: Personal Information Protection Act (個人情報保護法)

Primary sources: PPC [General Guidelines, section 3-8-1 and related request/complaint provisions](https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/guidelines_tsusoku/) and [APPI Q&A](https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/faq/APPI_QA/), reviewed October 27, 2026.

The guidelines address making retained-personal-data information accessible, including prompt answers to requests. Relevant matters include operator identification/address, purposes, request procedures, safeguards and complaints. An on-request arrangement may be possible for applicable information; it is not an automatic release from these obligations. Applicability depends on the operator and information handling, not simply whether the app has a financial backend.

**Blockers:** identify operator-held correspondence and purchase records, determine applicable duties/roles, approve a lawful identification/disclosure process, define proportionate verification and lawful response handling, review retention/safeguards and relevant foreign-provider arrangements. Do not assert every on-device record is remotely accessible, every provider is independent, or every provider record can be deleted by the developer. Do not require excessive personal documents merely to ask a privacy question.

## 4. United States and other markets

FTC guidance emphasizes accurate app claims and privacy practices: [Marketing Your Mobile App](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start). The drafts therefore avoid “no collection,” “fully anonymous,” guaranteed security and unqualified forecast/Lifetime claims. They retain mandatory consumer rights rather than inventing a blanket no-refunds or liability waiver.

Age-related review remains open. The [FTC's COPPA compliance guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business) addresses child-directed services and relevant actual-knowledge situations. The draft does not fabricate an age gate, age threshold policy, parental-consent system or a claim that a general finance app is automatically exempt.

State privacy/consumer laws and other launch-market requirements need an applicability assessment based on the operator and actual activity. No arbitrary US state is selected as governing law, no unverified statutory threshold is claimed, and no universal deadline or set of rights is promised. EU trader review is deferred because those storefronts are excluded; no App Store settings were changed here.

## 6. Lifetime promise and Apple purchase rules

The audited app explicitly includes current and future Local Pro features and excludes separately priced online/AI services. Its Lifetime SKU is non-consumable, not a renewable product. The draft Terms preserve this, including future Local Pro improvements. A general availability limitation must not be used to silently cancel that coverage. No price, free trial, permanent development obligation or perpetual platform support is invented.

Apple's [standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) remains the app license; this site supplies supplemental Terms. Review [subscription cancellation](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428), [refund requests](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) and the actual purchase confirmation. Do not automatically apply physical-goods return periods to digital purchases or declare all purchases non-refundable. Purchase restoration, cancellation and financial-data backup restoration remain distinct.
