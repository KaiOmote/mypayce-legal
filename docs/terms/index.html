2. Money planning, not a financial service

MyPayce records expenses, income, transfers, reimbursements, budgets, Card plans and recurring activity, and provides spending analysis, pacing, forecasts and hypothetical scenarios. It does not connect to your bank, hold your money, execute payments or confirm a payment merely because a reminder or projected event exists.

Results depend on your entries, settings, assumptions and available historical data. Forecasts and historical category estimates are estimates, not guarantees of income, account balances, investment returns, savings or future expenses. The app is not individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check important decisions against actual records and obtain qualified advice when appropriate.

3. Local records and backups

Financial data is stored locally. Review entries and imports for accuracy and keep suitable backups. A financial backup is unencrypted and can include archived/deleted records. Share it only with a trusted destination. Restore accepts supported backup formats and replaces financial data after confirmation; purchase restoration and financial-data restoration are separate operations.

Archiving and recoverable deletion are not permanent erasure. App removal, device changes or loss of backups can affect access to local records. The developer does not keep a server copy of your financial database from which to recover it. These practical limitations do not exclude responsibility or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

4. Free and Local Pro

The audited version keeps core financial tracking, budgets, planned/recurring activity, basic analytics and historical time-range browsing, CSV import, backup/restore and recovery available Free. The current-financial-month forecast is Free. Local Pro provides longer forecast horizons, Scenario tools and deeper comparative analysis, including advanced manual assumptions.

Monthly Pro, Annual Pro and Lifetime Local Pro grant the same Local Pro entitlement. Losing subscription access does not delete financial records. Premium results may be hidden while Free functionality remains available; purchase access is determined from Apple/RevenueCat information.

6. Monthly and annual subscriptions

Monthly Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription. Annual Pro is an auto-renewing yearly subscription. Each renews at the applicable renewal price unless cancelled through Apple Subscriptions in accordance with the purchase confirmation and Apple's rules.

Manage or cancel on your iPhone through Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops a future renewal rather than immediately removing access already paid for; expiration and any applicable billing grace period are determined by Apple/RevenueCat. Uninstalling MyPayce, clearing financial data or sending feedback does not cancel a subscription. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

7. Lifetime Local Pro

Lifetime Local Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not an auto-renewing subscription. It unlocks current and future Local Pro features. Separately priced online or AI services are not included.

The Lifetime purchase has no recurring subscription charge and is not sold as a time-limited subscription. Future improvements released as Local Pro are included. “Lifetime” does not promise perpetual development, compatibility with every future operating system or continued availability of third-party platforms. These limitations do not remove the promise to include future Local Pro features or any rights you have under applicable law.

8. Restoration and purchase identity

Use Restore Purchases on the MyPayce paywall to request restoration of eligible Apple purchases, generally using the Apple Account associated with the purchase. MyPayce has no account sign-in. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app-user identity; restoration can transfer an existing purchase to a new anonymous identity after reinstall, depending on the configured restore behavior and Apple's eligibility rules.

Restoration relies on the platform's purchase records and may require connectivity. It does not recreate missing financial records or import a financial backup. Existing subscription owners can use the app's subscription-management action or Apple's settings.
