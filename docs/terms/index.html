2. Money planning, not a financial service

MyPayce records expenses, income, transfers, reimbursements, budgets, Card plans and recurring activity, and provides spending analysis, pacing, forecasts and hypothetical scenarios. Results depend on your entries, settings, assumptions and available historical data. The app is not individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check important decisions against actual records and obtain qualified advice when appropriate.

3. Local records and backups

Financial data is stored locally. Review entries and imports for accuracy and keep suitable backups. A financial backup is unencrypted and can include archived/deleted records. Share it only with a trusted destination. Archiving and recoverable deletion are not permanent erasure. App removal, device changes or loss of backups can affect access to local records. The developer does not keep a server copy of your financial database from which to recover it. These practical limitations do not exclude responsibility or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.
