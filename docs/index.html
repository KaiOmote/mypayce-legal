Local-first, with clear boundaries

MyPayce helps you record expenses and income, plan recurring activity, manage budgets and explore financial forecasts. Financial records and calculations stay in the app's local storage. MyPayce has no financial-data account or cloud-sync service.

Local-first does not mean no third-party processing: Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases, Expo supports app updates, and information you choose to email or export leaves the app through your selected service.

An international app

The planned initial App Store distribution is broad and international, excluding the 27 European Union member countries pending a separate trader-compliance review. Availability is determined by the App Store listing. This public website is not geoblocked.

These drafts are for an international audience, with Japanese and US requirements as primary research reference points. They do not claim compliance with every jurisdiction or assume EU storefront exclusion removes every potentially applicable privacy obligation.

Questions are welcome

For support, feedback, privacy questions or operator-disclosure inquiries, email kaiomote10+MyPayce@gmail.com. The same mailbox handles in-app feedback. Please do not send financial databases, passwords or full payment-card details.

MyPayce is the public app name used by an individual developer. The legal operator's identification and disclosure arrangements are publication-review items; this name must not be mistaken for an incorporated entity.
