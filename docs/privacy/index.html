1. About MyPayce

This policy covers the MyPayce iOS app and this legal-information website. MyPayce is developed and operated by an individual developer. “MyPayce” is the app name, not a company name.

For privacy questions or information about the operator, contact kaiomote10+MyPayce@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

MyPayce stores financial information in a local SQLite database: accounts and balances, Card settings and payment plans, categories, transactions, merchants and notes, transfers, reimbursements, budgets, planned activity, recurring rules and financial settings. Identifiers and archive/deletion states are also retained.

Local preferences include language, onboarding completion, remembered Scenario Setup, reminder settings and read/dismissal history, and Developer Mode. A remembered Scenario Setup contains configuration, not an old financial result.

The app calculates budgets, pacing, spending analytics, forecasts and hypothetical scenarios on-device. It does not connect to your bank or upload these financial records to a MyPayce server. No financial-data login, cloud synchronization or AI service is implemented in MyPayce.

3. Apple purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store payments. MyPayce uses RevenueCat to retrieve products, validate purchase access, restore purchases and maintain Pro entitlement status. RevenueCat receives purchase-related information such as product and transaction identifiers, receipt/transaction information, subscription status and a randomly generated app-user identifier, together with technical request information used by its service.

The SDK uses an anonymous purchase identity without a MyPayce login. “Anonymous” here means no developer-supplied account identity; it does not mean the purchase information cannot be linked to a device or purchase history. The developer may view purchase/customer records in RevenueCat's dashboard.

MyPayce does not pass your financial SQLite records, account names, balances, categories, financial transactions or Scenario inputs to RevenueCat. It does not request your full Apple payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple and RevenueCat process their own records under their applicable terms and policies. See Apple's privacy information and RevenueCat's purchase-data disclosure guidance.

Restoring purchases can associate an existing Apple purchase with a new anonymous RevenueCat identity after reinstall. It does not restore your local financial database.

4. Software updates and technical information

The app is configured to use Expo's EAS Update service for compatible software updates. Update checks and downloads contact Expo infrastructure and can involve network information such as an IP address, app/runtime and operating-system information, and randomized update-related identifiers. They do not upload your financial database. See Expo's privacy policy.

MyPayce does not integrate separate advertising, behavioral-tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple, RevenueCat and Expo may still process service or technical information under their own policies and settings. RevenueCat also provides purchase analytics.

5. Local reminders

Reminders are generated from recurring activity and scheduled locally through iOS. No remote push server, push-token registration or financial-data upload is used for them. Notification permission is requested when you explicitly enable reminders or request a native Developer Mode test.

System reminders show the activity type, recurring-rule name and due date. MyPayce does not automatically add transaction amounts, balances or other transaction metadata. A rule name you enter may itself contain sensitive information and can appear on the lock screen, depending on iOS preview settings.

You can switch off OS reminders and manage iOS notification permissions. The in-app inbox continues to work independently of OS delivery. Read and dismissal information stays local. Reminder history does not prove a notification was delivered or money was paid.

6. Feedback and voluntary sharing

Feedback opens your email application with a category-based subject and the developer's contact address. Opening the composer does not send a message. The app does not automatically attach financial data or diagnostics.

If you send email, the operator receives your sender address, message and anything you choose to include. Your email provider and the recipient mailbox provider (Gmail) process the correspondence. These messages may be used to answer questions, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or an unsolicited financial database.

Developer Mode offers a diagnostic database export through the system share sheet. This is a user-directed export, not automatic crash reporting; the file can contain sensitive financial records.

7. Backups, imports and device services

CSV imports are processed by the app. Financial backups are standalone unencrypted SQLite files containing financial records, settings, relationships, and archived/deleted records. They exclude presentation preferences such as language and remembered Scenario Setup. Anyone with a backup can inspect its contents.

MyPayce does not automatically upload these files. The file provider, share destination or recipient you choose may store or synchronize them, including through cloud services. Keep backups in a trusted location. Imported files and exports you retain outside the app are managed separately from the live database.

App data may also be included in device or iCloud backups according to your system settings. This is separate from MyPayce providing a cloud-sync service. See Apple's backup information.

8. Retention and deletion

Local financial records remain while you keep the app's data. Archiving an entity or deleting a transaction usually retains a recoverable record; it is not secure erasure. Removing the app's local data, offloading the app, deleting an exported backup and deleting a device backup are different actions. Copies retained by a file provider or another recipient are not removed by deleting a record in MyPayce.

The in-app inbox retains up to 50 real historical reminders, with up to 10 separate Developer Mode test entries. Read/dismissal metadata supports deduplication; obsolete dismissal information is pruned by the app's generation/reference rules. Clear all dismisses retained history without cancelling future real reminders.

Purchase, update, email and hosting records have separate retention practices and may be retained for service operation, security or applicable legal obligations. The operator cannot guarantee complete deletion of independently controlled provider records or secure erasure from device storage and backups.

9. Privacy questions and requests

Email kaiomote10+MyPayce@gmail.com with a description of your request and the information concerned. Depending on applicable law and the information held, requests may concern access, purposes of use, correction, deletion, stopping certain uses or disclosures, or a complaint. No particular subject line or paid purchase is required to ask.

Provide only enough information to help locate the relevant correspondence or purchase-related record. Additional, proportionate verification may be necessary to protect against unauthorized requests. Do not send identity documents, bank statements, financial databases or passwords unless a lawful, necessary and suitable process has first been agreed.

The operator cannot remotely inspect, recover, edit or erase financial data stored only on your device. Local records and files must be managed on your device and in any backup service you use. For information under the operator's control, valid requests must be handled under applicable law, including appropriate coordination with service providers. Some records are independently controlled by Apple or other providers, and some may be retained where legally required; complete third-party deletion cannot be guaranteed.

Deleting the app or requesting data deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Use Apple's subscription settings separately. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law. If a request cannot be fulfilled in whole or in part, the response will explain the relevant limitations.

10. Security and international processing

Local data uses the app's device storage and operating-system protections; no application-level encryption guarantee is made. Exported backups are not password-protected by MyPayce. Protect device access and share files carefully.

Apple, RevenueCat, Expo, email providers and hosting providers may process their respective information outside your country. Their privacy policies explain their practices. For questions about MyPayce's use of these services, contact the operator at the address below.

11. This website

This website adds no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, forms, local storage or third-party embedded resources. Language selection uses ordinary links. External provider-policy links make requests only when you choose to follow them.

The site is intended for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub documents that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. Hosting is therefore not a “no collection” service. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.
